Deep Cuts

A good song deserves your whole attention.

For the next few minutes, there's nothing else you need to open.

There's a particular kind of evening when one song is enough. You put it on, settle into your chair, and let the room go a little quiet around it. No skipping ahead. No next thing.

That's the feeling we want to make room for here. A soft-rock harmony you missed the first dozen times. A guitar part disappearing into a shoegaze haze. A music video that builds a small world of its own.

Let the picture play, too.

A music video can change the way you hear a song. A lingering shot gives a line more space. A sudden cut makes a familiar beat feel different.

The best rabbit holes start with a small detail you can't quite leave alone.

Until the first story docks, find a track you love. Give it a few uninterrupted minutes. See what you hear this time.
